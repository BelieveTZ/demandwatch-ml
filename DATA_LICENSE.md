# Data attribution

Fanaee-T, H. (2013). **Bike Sharing** [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5W894

Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/275/bike+sharing+dataset

UCI identifies this dataset as licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The hourly CSV describes Capital Bikeshare rental counts in 2011–2012. The raw source
is downloaded on demand and is not committed to this repository. The included JSON
replay and CSV evaluation contain transformed/selected observations, added historical
features, model predictions, uncertainty bounds and evaluation statistics. Those
modifications are part of DemandWatch ML; they are not endorsed by the dataset creator.

Download location and SHA-256 checksums are recorded by the pipeline. The pinned
`hour.csv` SHA-256 is:
